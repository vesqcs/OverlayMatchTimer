# v2.0.3 – Root causes of the 2.0.2 field failures (Examino, InPlay, local player)

**Date:** 17 Nov 2026
**Branch / PR:** `cursor/v203-fullscreen-return-ccd4`, stacked on PR #13 (`cursor/v201-panel-skips-ccd4`). Not merged.
**Comparison point:** v1.2.0 (`811be7b`). It behaves better than 2.0.2, but it was used only for comparison. Nothing was copied from it wholesale.

## Summary

| Host | Why 2.0.2 failed | What 1.2.0 did differently | 2.0.3 fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Examino DVR | Auto-VOD kept clicking Source → VOD. Each click makes the player reload the stream. | Tried a bounded number of times (1 + 6 retries), then stopped. | At most one Source click per page load, bounded retries, no document-wide observer. |
| InPlay /schedule | Our bar and panel painted **above** InPlay's schedule curtain and its up arrow. | Bar was not at the arrow position, but the panel still painted over the open schedule. | The HUD now stays inside the player's own layer. All host UI above the player paints and clicks above us. |
| All hosts | After a `<video>` was replaced, our listeners stayed on the dead element (timeline, auto-hide, buttons, speed keys followed the old video). | Function did not exist in 1.2.0 (added in 1.4.2). | A replaced video always goes through the normal teardown and boot. |
| Local player | `play()` promise not caught (uncaught AbortError). Possible decode stall after Alt+Tab in fullscreen. | Same uncaught `play()`. | Every `play()` we call is caught. Decode is re-asserted after focus or visibility return and after seeks, only while already playing in fullscreen. |

## Root causes in plain words

### 1. Examino: the player flashes and never opens (auto-VOD loop)

From 1.2.2 onwards, auto-VOD ran from a `MutationObserver` on the whole document. Every DOM change restarted the attempt counter at zero.

On the real DVR popup, the extension can't read back that VOD is selected:
- the URL is `/play/dvr/…`;
- the Source row does not show the literal word "VOD".

So every attempt decided "still DVR" and clicked VOD again. Each click makes OpenPlayer pause, rebuild the media and call `play()`. That rebuild changes the DOM, which fires the observer, which starts the next attempt.

Result: the player keeps reloading (the flashing), never settles, and the page logs "play() request was interrupted by a call to pause()" every time. Those errors come from **OpenPlayer's** `play()`, triggered by our clicks. The Mixed Content warnings for `http://10.217.82.32:8001` are the host's own and unrelated.

1.2.0 behaved better because it made one attempt after 1.2 s plus at most 6 retries, with no observer.

**2.0.3:**
- one delayed attempt and at most 6 retries;
- **at most one Source click per page load**, whether or not it can be confirmed;
- no observer.

The settings menu is opened only during those bounded attempts.

### 2. Examino: native-chrome hider work

`hideExaminoNativeChrome()` reads `innerText` of every `div` (forces layout). It ran 50 ms after any DOM change, including our own HUD clock text updates several times a second. It now ignores changes inside our HUD and runs at most once per second.

The "never hide a node that contains the video" guard from 2.0.2 is kept. CSS and JS both refuse to hide any ancestor of the `<video>`.

### 3. InPlay: our bar and panel cover the schedule and the up arrow

InPlay's `/schedule` page puts its schedule curtain over a full-viewport player:
- `.table-expanded` has z-index 3;
- the open curtain's up arrow sits at the bottom centre (z-index 4);
- the collapsed arrow sits at the top centre (z-index 6).

Our `#omt-root` uses a very large z-index so it beats the player's own controls. No ancestor between it and the page formed a stacking context, so that z-index competed with the **whole page**: our timeline bar sat on top of the up arrow.
- Clicks on the arrow hit our bar.
- The curtain couldn't be closed, in or out of fullscreen.
- Our panel painted over the schedule list.

The 2.0.1 "visibility guard", which hid and showed our HUD as layout changed, added churn on every curtain and fullscreen change on top of that.

**2.0.3:**
- `isolation: isolate` on the player box we attach to, restored on teardown. Our HUD still beats the player's controls, but anything InPlay stacks above the player now paints and receives clicks above us. No host node is hidden, moved or resized.
- The visibility guard is removed entirely.
- Host-control hiding is gated on a class set once when our HUD mounts and removed on teardown. It is never toggled by observers.

### 4. A replaced `<video>` kept our listeners on the old element (2.0.x regression)

`ensureLiveVideo()` (added in 1.4.2) and the Examino click handler did `vid = newVideo` directly when the page swapped its `<video>`. Our per-video listeners (time, play/pause, mouse-move) stayed on the dead element. The 1.5 s SPA monitor then saw "current video == vid" and never re-bound.

After a Source switch on Examino, this left the timeline, auto-hide, play button and speed keys following the old video. 2.0.2's tests missed it only because its overlay Source select was stuck on "Loading…", so the switch never actually happened.

**2.0.3:** both paths now go through the same teardown and boot that the monitor uses. The Source select shows DVR/VOD immediately instead of "Loading…".

### 5. Local player: uncaught AbortError, Alt+Tab fullscreen seeks

- Every `play()` our code calls now goes through `safePlay()`, which catches AbortError and NotAllowedError. This covers play/pause buttons, Space, video click, ShuttleXpress button and auto-resume.
- After returning to the window (focus or `visibilitychange` → visible), and after keyboard or ShuttleXpress seeks, we call `play()` once **only if the video is fullscreen and already playing**. It never unpauses a user pause and cannot loop.
- The ShuttleXpress skip keeps its original direct time write. HID input, jog and ring are unchanged.

## GPU / decoder audit (honest answer about the black-screen freeze)

| Item | Load | Status in 2.0.3 |
|---|---|---|
| `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` on the panel over live video | Re-blurs every decoded frame. Worst in fullscreen. Also keeps Chrome off its cheap video-overlay path. | **Removed** (solid panel background). |
| Examino auto-VOD reload loop | Continuous decoder teardown and setup plus page `play()`/`pause()` churn. | **Fixed** (at most one click). |
| 2.0.1 visibility guard | Show/hide churn on layout changes. | **Removed**. |
| Examino chrome hider `innerText` scans | Forced layout on every DOM change. | **Throttled to ≤ 1/s**; ignores our HUD. |
| ShuttleXpress ring | 20 seeks per second **while the ring is held** (original mechanism, also in 1.2.0). | Unchanged. It is the heaviest decoder load we create, but only during user input. |
| Animation-frame loops | One single-frame layout callback when opening the More panel; no continuous loop. | — |

**Honest conclusion:** our code could put real extra load on the GPU and decoder. That load came from the panel blur over fullscreen video, the 2.0.1 visibility churn, and on Examino the reload loop. All three are gone in 2.0.3.

A hard freeze with a black screen that needs a power-off is, however, a GPU driver or OS-level hang. A web page or extension can stress the GPU but cannot by itself hang Windows. The most likely explanation is a Chrome/GPU-driver hang triggered under that extra load.

If it happens again on 2.0.3, the useful checks are:
- the GPU driver version and any "display driver stopped responding" events in Windows Event Viewer;
- `chrome://gpu`.

## Chrome 157 on the work PC (157.0.8103.79, Windows 11 25H2)

Vasco reports that 1.2.0 and the original also misbehave now. I checked the 156 → 157 changes for anything touching our areas. These are public release notes and third-party change trackers, not a test on his machine:

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| MAIN-world content scripts | No change in 157. MV3 MAIN-world injection is unchanged. |
| Autoplay policy | No change (muted autoplay still allowed). |
| Background / hidden video pausing | Nothing listed in 157. Trackers report "Media and GPU: unchanged". |
| IntersectionObserver in fullscreen | No change listed. 2.0.3 no longer uses IntersectionObserver for the HUD anyway. |
| CSS `:has()` | No change listed. Only used in our "don't hide the video's ancestor" guards. |
| **HTTPS-First "balanced" mode on by default (new in 157)** | Upgrades or warns `http://` *navigations* to public sites. Private IPs such as `10.x.x.x` are exempt. Unlikely to be the cause, but it is new in exactly this version. |
| **Local Network Access** (permission for public HTTPS sites loading from private IPs) | Examino loads from `http://10.217.82.32:8001`, a private address. If that site's "Local network access" permission is *Ask* or *Blocked* on the work PC, media/stream requests to that IP fail, with or without our extension. That would explain the player not opening even on 1.2.0. **This is the most plausible outside factor.** |
| Field trials from the variations server | Can switch features on per machine. Can't be ruled out remotely. |

Nothing in 157 explains InPlay; that matches an InPlay DOM/CSS change. 2.0.3 was checked against InPlay's current production CSS (Nov 2026), and that CSS is what made the curtain arrow collide with our bar.

**Suggested checks on the work PC:**
1. Open Examino with the extension **disabled**. If the player still fails, the cause is outside our code.
2. Click the lock icon → Site settings, and check "Local network access" for the Examino origin. Set it to Allow and retry.
3. In the console, look for messages mentioning *Local Network Access* or *Private Network* next to the 10.217.82.32 requests.
4. On 2.0.2, many `[OMT] Auto-switching Examino source to VOD` lines in the console confirm the loop from root cause 1.

## What was dropped or simplified

- **Visibility guard (2.0.1–2.0.2):** removed on all hosts. The HUD no longer hides itself when a curtain closes. On InPlay the stacking fix keeps the curtain and arrow on top; on Examino the HUD sits inside the player.
- **Auto-VOD retry-forever behaviour:** now one Source click per page load. If the page can't confirm VOD, the overlay shows the last choice and the analyst can switch manually.
- **Glass blur behind the panel:** replaced by a solid dark background.

Kept unchanged:
- **2.0.x features:** popup copy, custom skip times, panel size and compact mode, YouTube-style bar, fullscreen button.
- **Original mechanisms:** player hooks, time/PDT reading, adapters, intervals, ShuttleXpress.
