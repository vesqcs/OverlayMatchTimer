4 MATCH CLOCK SYNCHRONIZATION 
Once the video file is successfully loaded onto the dashboard canvas, you must calibrate 
the running match clock. This process establishes an absolute time anchor, ensuring your 
tactical analysis is synced to the match timeline. 
  
4.1 FINDING THE KICK-OFF FRAME 
 Use the timeline slider, the control panel buttons, or the shortcuts to navigate through 
the video. 
  
 Locate the exact kick-off frame. 
Note: This follows the exact same operational workflow as anchoring a kick-off inside the 
SoccerClient PMT tool.  
 Pause the video precisely on that kick-off frame. 
  
4.2 SELECTING THE MATCH HALF 
Before saving the synchronization anchor, you must specify which period of the match you 
are calibrating. 
  
 In the Setup Panel, navigate to section 2. MATCH HALF. 
  
Click the dropdown menu to select the active period:

1st Half: Select this for the start of the match. The timer will begin running exactly at 00:00. 
  
2nd Half: Select this for the second half. The timer will begin running exactly at 45:00.

Custom 1: Starts at 90:00 by default. Use this for Extra-Time periods or edit the input

4.3 CALIBRATING THE CLOCK ("SET KICK-OFF TIME") 
Section 3. WHISTLE TIME (HR : MIN : SEC) offers three distinct calibration workflows 
depending on your live environment: 
 
 
FIGURE 8 - WHISTLE TIME CALIBRATION SECTION 
Method A: Standard Automated Calibration (Zero Offset) 
Pause the video on the exact kick-off frame, leave the Whistle Time input boxes 
completely empty (at 0), and click the bright green SET KICK-OFF TIME button. The HUD 
overlay will instantly capture this frame as your 00:00 baseline reference point. 
  
Method B: Manual Calibration & Micro-Adjustments (Offset Fine-Tuning) 
If you notice that your running overlay clock is slightly misaligned (e.g., running 2 seconds 
late compared to SoccerClient), you do not need to clear everything and start over. You 
can manually edit the offset values into the HR : MIN : SEC boxes and click the SET KICK-
OFF TIME button after changing the values. 
  
Example: If an anchor at 1 minute and 6 seconds into the video needs adjustment, you 
can manually edit the input to 0 - 1 - 8. This forces the timer to sync its starting minute 
parameter precisely to the 8-second mark of the video instead.

Method C: Remote Collaborative Synchronization 
When coordinating workflows where the home team analyst has already completed the 
official timeline setup, they can share their exact anchor parameters with the away team 
analyst. The away analyst simply types those shared parameters directly into the HR : MIN 
: SEC field inputs and clicks SET KICK-OFF TIME. This ensures both analysts operate on 
perfectly mirrored timelines down to the exact same second. 
  
4.4 WIPING SESSION CACHE AND RESETTING (THE RED "X" 
BUTTON) 
The small Red "X" Button located to the right of the SET KICK-OFF TIME bar serves two 
vital purposes: 
 
 
FIGURE 10 - RESET SYNCHRONIZATION BUTTON 
Accidental Click Correction: If you calibrate the clock on an incorrect video frame or 
choose the wrong half, click the red X to clear the active anchor data and re-calibrate. 
  
Data Persistence & New Game Clears (F5 Protection): To protect your work against 
unexpected disruptions, the tool automatically caches your active match parameters inside 
the browser session. If you refresh the page (F5), your synchronized clock remains 
completely safe. However, when you start working on a completely new match, the 
browser will still hold the cached timer data from the previous game. Clicking the Red "X" 
Button is mandatory at the start of a new session to flush the entire browser storage clean 
and reset all internal master clocks back to zero.

5 ADVANCED MATCH NAVIGATION & CONTROLS 
The navigation controls are designed to match the workflows used in VLC Media Player 
and Xeatre, reducing the learning curve for existing analysts. 
  
5.1 THE DRAGGABLE & AUTO-HIDING CONTROL PANEL 
The floating Control Panel at the bottom of the video canvas provides access to the main 
playback controls. It includes two features designed to improve usability during live and 
post-match workflows. 
  
Fully Draggable Interface: The entire panel is floating. You can click, hold, and drag the 
Control Panel to move it to any area of the screen. 
  
2.5-Second Auto-Hide Feature: To guarantee a completely unobstructed view of the pitch 
during playback, the Control Panel automatically fades away after 2.5 seconds of mouse 
inactivity. It reappears the moment you move your cursor.

5.5 SHUTTLEXPRESS HARDWARE INTEGRATION 
(COLLABORATIVE REVIEW CONTROL)  
In a standard live match environment, workstations are split between at least two analysts 
(Home and Away team). While the lower-middle screen displays the live match feed, the 
upper-middle monitor is dedicated to replaying and dissecting controversial events. 
For Tier 12 or non-GVN games, video control on this upper monitor was traditionally 
locked to the Home analyst's PC, meaning the Away analyst could not control the upper-
middle video like in GVN. 
Built with native support for the Contour ShuttleXpress USB device via the WebHID API, a 
single physical controller can be connected to the Home PC, placed centrally on the desk, 
and used by both analysts to execute hands-on, tactile control of video playback without 
ever touching a keyboard or mouse.

6 WORKING WITH ONLINE STREAMS

them directly into Section 1B of the Overlay MatchTimer. This integration is explicitly 
designed for matches that lack an on-screen transmission timer, or for games where 
analysts prefer to leverage the advanced review ecosystem of the Overlay MatchTimer 
such as dynamic period bookmarking and central ShuttleXpress hardware control. 
  
6.1 EXTRACTING AND LOADING URLS FROM XEATRE 
 
 Authentication & Selection  
Open Xeatre in your browser, log in to your analyst account, and locate the video of the 
match assigned. 
 
 Launching the DVR Player  
Click the DVR button corresponding to the match, exactly as you would during a normal 
game. This will launch the platform's dedicated DVR stream in a separate pop-up window. 
 
 
FIGURE 19 - OPEN XEATRE DVR POPUP 
 
 Capturing the Stream URL  
Click directly on the address bar of the newly opened DVR pop-up window to highlight the 
complete URL string. Copy it to your clipboard (Ctrl + C).

6.3 INSTALLING AND ACTIVATING THE EXTENSION FOR 
INPLAY 
 
Unlike Xeatre and Examino platforms, the Inplay integration requires a manual local 
installation of the browser extension in Developer Mode. This is highly beneficial for 
matches where Inplay is the only source, as it bypasses the platform's small, difficult-to-
use native mouse controls. It fully unlocks the exact same keyboard shortcuts used in the 
local HTML app, alongside all other Overlay MatchTimer features. Once loaded from the 
local network storage (NAS), the extension will automatically inject the overlay directly into 
any active Inplay video review player. 
 
 Open Extension Management 
Click on the three dots icon (More Actions) in the top-right corner of your browser toolbar. 
Navigate to Extensions and select Manage Extensions (alternatively, type 
chrome://extensions directly into your browser's address bar and press Enter). 
 
 
FIGURE 25 - OPEN CHROME EXTENSIONS 
 
 Enable Developer Mode  
Once on the Extensions management page, locate the Developer mode toggle switch in 
the top-right corner and switch it ON.
